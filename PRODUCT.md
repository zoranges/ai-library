# PRODUCT.md

## Product Purpose

AI Electronic Library System (AI电子图书馆) — an intelligent digital reading platform for K-12 schools in Malaysia's multilingual education environment. Combines e-book reading, AI-assisted learning, reading behavior tracking, and gamified incentives. Two surfaces: student-facing reading platform and admin management dashboard.

## Users

- **Students (primary)**: K-12 readers who browse, read, take quizzes, earn points, and use AI assistance. Multilingual (English, Malay, Mandarin, Tamil). Need engaging, intuitive interfaces that make reading feel rewarding.
- **School Administrators**: Manage student reading data, view reports, export records. Need efficient data-dense views with clear actions.
- **Super Administrators**: Full platform control — schools, books, users, statistics. Need powerful management tools with role switching.
- **Teachers**: Monitor class reading progress. Lightweight oversight role.
- **Parents**: View associated student reading activity. Read-only, occasional use.

## Brand

- **Tone**: Trustworthy yet warm. A public education tool that feels modern and inviting, not bureaucratic. Think "world-class digital library" not "school management software."
- **Personality**: Intelligent, encouraging, inclusive, calm.
- **Visual direction**: International enterprise SaaS quality — the polish level of Linear, Notion, or Vercel, but with an education-appropriate warmth. Not cold tech, not playful childishness.

## Register

**product** — design serves the product. Clarity, efficiency, and usability over decorative expression. The reading experience is paramount; admin tools prioritize data density and action clarity.

## Anti-references

- Generic "edtech" templates with cartoon mascots and primary colors
- SaaS dashboards with hero-metric big-number cards and gradient accents
- Cookie-cutter admin panels with identical card grids
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, or neon-on-dark crypto aesthetics
- Chinese-government-style blue-and-white bureaucratic interfaces

## Strategic Principles

1. **Reading first**: Every design choice should make reading more enjoyable and accessible
2. **Multilingual by default**: The UI must feel native in all four languages, not like English with translations bolted on
3. **Calm intelligence**: AI features should feel like a helpful tutor, not a chatbot — subtle, contextual, non-intrusive
4. **Progressive disclosure**: Students see simple, inviting interfaces; power users (admins) access depth through filters and expandable sections
5. **Data with empathy**: Reading statistics should motivate, not surveil — celebrate growth, don't rank shame
6. **Accessibility as baseline**: This is a school tool. WCAG AA minimum. High contrast, clear focus states, keyboard navigation.
